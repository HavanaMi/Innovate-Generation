About Us

Innovate Generation is a Community Interest Company (CIC) based in London, UK. We organize cultural and creative events including fashion shows, art and sculpture auctions, gallery exhibitions, photography showcases, and public workshops. Our aim is to connect talent with opportunity — across all disciplines, levels of experience, and backgrounds.

We support individuals — whether young artists 16-25 years old, unemployed, freelance, or emerging — by creating opportunities to sell art, handmade products, or perform live. This includes collaborations with galleries, fashion venues, and auction houses who are seeking new talent and fresh perspectives.

Our work is made possible through the engagement of sponsors, institutions, cultural partners, and companies who believe in social impact through creativity. We welcome B2B partnerships, CSR initiatives, and supporters who want to fund artistic, musical, or performance-based initiatives.

Our events are also open to the public — from gallery exhibitions and fashion shows to cultural auctions and creative markets. Whether you're looking to buy unique pieces, enjoy an inspiring show, or support local talent, there's a place for you in our community.

Our Social Mission

Innovate Generation exists to promote creativity as a tool for social inclusion, opportunity, and personal growth. Through cultural production and access to real audiences, we help creative people thrive in their craft and gain professional momentum.

About the Project
