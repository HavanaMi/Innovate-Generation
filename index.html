Innovate Generation UK-based CIC hosting creative events — fashion shows, art and photography exhibitions — offering the public a chance to buy youth-made works. We promote artists, especially ages 14–25, by giving them a platform to showcase their talent.

We Offer creative production services, including video and photography, tailored workshops, and individual training. Our work supports young talent by providing skills, guidance, and opportunities to present their creativity to wider audiences.

Our Goal

Our aim is to create collaboration, offer professional development, and promote young talent across artistic disciplines. We offer free mentoring, creative workshops, and the chance for participants to sell their work through exhibitions and events. Whether it’s fashion, photography, painting, sculpture or more – our programs are here to support emerging creators in building skills, confidence, and new futures.
